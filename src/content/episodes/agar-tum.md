*Agar Tum…* is a little journey through the thoughts we keep to ourselves, the moments we wish could last longer, and the quiet happiness of having someone who makes life feel a little more beautiful.

It's about love, unspoken wishes, finding comfort in someone's presence, and discovering that sometimes we don't need everything we ever wished for. Sometimes, one peaceful evening and a few heartfelt moments are enough.

After almost two years, I'm bringing Khwaab-e-Surkhaab back with this little piece of my heart. A poem written and narrated by me, with a little music to accompany the words.
